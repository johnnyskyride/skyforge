# SkyForge

Analog instrument by [@johnnyskyride](https://x.com/johnnyskyride).

## Play

Download **SkyForge.html** (or open `index.html`).

1. Open it in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge
2. Click to arm audio
3. Play the on-screen keys, or your computer keyboard (Z–M / Q–P, space to sustain)

No install. Send the file to anyone.

## Ableton

Arm, play a part, hit **Bounce** for a WAV, drop it on a track. That is the DAW path.

Halloween knobs the haunt. Presets: Init, Bass, Lead, Pad, Pluck, Keys, Haunt.

SF-33.
